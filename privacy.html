Privacy Policy

Tormentdev Stats · Last updated: October 13, 2026

Tormentdev Stats ("the App") is a personal, non-commercial tool developed by Tormentdev. It is used only by its developer to view statistics of the Tormentdev accounts on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and itch.io in a single dashboard. This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is handled.

1. Data we access

When the account owner signs in with TikTok, the App may access, through the official TikTok API and only with the permissions explicitly granted:

  • Basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID).
  • Public account statistics (follower count, following count, likes, video count).
  • The list of the account's own videos and their public metrics (views, likes, comments, shares).

The App does not access private messages, contacts, location, payment information or any data from other TikTok users.

2. How we use the data

Data is used solely to display statistics to the account owner. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any automated decision-making.

3. Storage and security

  • Data and access tokens are stored locally on the developer's own device and are not uploaded to any third-party server.
  • Access tokens are kept private and are never exposed publicly or shared.
  • The App only requests read-only permissions and never posts, edits or deletes content.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.

5. Data retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed to show statistics. You can revoke the App's access at any time from TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions. Upon revocation, the stored data can be deleted on request.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children and is not intended to be used by anyone under 18.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. Changes will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

For any question about this policy, contact: tormentdev.itch.io
